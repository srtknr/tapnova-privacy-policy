TAP NOVA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

TAP NOVA (“the game”, “we”) is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what information the game uses.

1. No account

The game does not require registration, email, or a player profile. We do not operate a player database.

2. Saved on your device

Progress, settings, stars, and purchase flags are stored locally on your device (app preferences). This data stays on the device unless you delete the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads) unless you have purchased No Ads. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, and approximate location to provide and measure ads. On iOS 14 and later we ask for tracking permission. You can decline; ads may still appear in a less personalized form.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases

No Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Apple or Google. Purchase receipts are validated by the store. We do not receive your full payment card details.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The game does not include a third-party analytics or crash SDK at this time.

6. Children

The game is a general-audience arcade title and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking
  • Android: Google Settings → Ads
  • Delete the app to remove local save data
  • Restore Purchases recovers a previous No Ads purchase

8. Contact

Use the support email published on the TAP NOVA App Store or Google Play listing.
